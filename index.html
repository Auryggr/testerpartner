QUESTIONS

A few things you might be wondering.

What happens after I complete the questionnaire?

We use your answers to investigate relevant public evidence and prepare preliminary evidence. We share what we found, along with uncertainties, before the Mapping Session. This evidence is a starting point to examine together, not a validated conclusion.

Why do you research before the session?

Your client challenge gives us a focused question to investigate. The goal is to arrive with preliminary evidence and open questions, so we can examine both in light of business context and constraints.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Only the short questionnaire. Share the client decision you’re facing and enough context to guide our initial research.

Will you always find customer reviews?

No. Reviews are one useful source when they exist, but the method is broader. We may use public customer comments, testimonials, product pages, competitor claims or other relevant public evidence. The source depends on the question.

What is preliminary evidence?

Information gathered from an initial review of relevant sources. It may suggest patterns, tensions or unanswered questions, but it does not establish validated conclusions. We examine its relevance and limitations together in the Mapping Session.

Why is the Mapping Session free?

Because this first step helps determine whether there is a meaningful opportunity to pursue and whether structured experimentation would actually reduce uncertainty.

Does every Mapping Session lead to a partnership?

No. The Mapping Session helps us understand the client challenge and assess whether continued investigation or experimentation would be useful. A partnership is only considered if it makes sense for both sides.

What if a client does not have enough traffic for A/B testing?

We assess test feasibility and realistic timelines before recommending experiments. Customer research, funnel diagnosis, behavioral analysis, confirmed fixes or monitoring may be better next steps.

Will the first session deliver a complete strategy?

No. Preliminary research helps us begin with evidence; the 15-minute Mapping Session is for understanding the client's context, constraints, assumptions and next questions.

Can our agency apply this with multiple clients?

Yes. We start with one selected client challenge, then can scope additional accounts separately. The free Mapping Session focuses on one client.

Are you a DTC brand rather than an agency?

You’re welcome here, too. While TesterPartner is primarily built for agencies, we also work directly with ecommerce brands facing growth decisions. Bring one challenge to a free Mapping Session, and we’ll explore the evidence together.

Do you guarantee conversion uplift?

No. Our approach supports better-informed decisions and honest learning, not guaranteed outcomes or statistical certainty.
