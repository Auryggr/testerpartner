Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

The short version
  • We collect only what we need to prepare and run your Positioning Breakdown: your brief, your name, work email, company website and booking details.
  • Sessions are recorded and transcribed only with your prior agreement. You can say no and still have your session.
  • If you decide not to continue after the free session, we delete your session data within 72 hours.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time: hello@testerpartner.com.

1. Who is responsible for your data

TesterPartner is a trade name operated by Aurysmar Guerra, an independent professional based in Buenos Aires, Argentina (“TesterPartner”, “we”, “us”). Aurysmar Guerra is the data controller responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Contact for any privacy question or request: hello@testerpartner.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, complete the Positioning Breakdown brief, book and attend a session, receive our deliverables (such as the Opportunity Brief), receive emails from us, or become a client for a Testing Strategy Session or other paid engagement.

Our services are offered to businesses and professionals. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your brief: your answers about the decision or challenge your team is facing, what makes it difficult and what outcome you want.
  • Contact and business details: your name, work email and company (or client) website.
  • Booking details: the date and time you choose and calendar information needed to send the invitation.
  • Correspondence: emails and messages you exchange with us.
  • Session content: what is discussed during the session and, with your agreement, its recording and transcript (see section 6).

Please do not send us passwords, payment card details, confidential customer lists or sensitive personal information (such as health, political or religious data). We do not need them to provide the service.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and request logs to deliver and secure the website.
  • Analytics data: if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 collects information about how you use the website (pages viewed, referral source, interactions). See section 8.

Public information used in our research

To prepare your session, we review publicly available information related to your question, such as public product reviews, customer comments, testimonials, product pages and competitor messaging. This content can occasionally include names or usernames of the people who wrote it. We use it only to identify recurring themes; we do not try to identify, profile or contact those individuals, and in our deliverables we paraphrase or anonymize what they wrote.

5. Anonymized examples

We may use what we learn from past analyses to create anonymized examples that illustrate our method, such as the example on our website. These examples never identify you, your company or your clients, and never include recordings, transcripts or quotes from your session. We will not publish a case study that identifies you or your company without your separate written permission. If you prefer that your analysis is not used even in anonymized form, tell us at any time.

6. Session recordings and transcripts

  • We ask for your agreement before any session is recorded, both when you book and again at the start of the call, before recording begins.
  • Recording is optional. If you do not agree, the session takes place without recording and we take written notes instead. Declining does not affect your access to the session.
  • If other people join the call, we ask all participants before recording.
  • We use Laxis, an AI-powered meeting assistant, to record, transcribe and summarize sessions. Its participation in the call is visible to all participants.
  • Recordings and transcripts are used only to prepare your deliverables and follow-up. We never publish them or use them in marketing.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time, including during the call, and ask us to delete the recording.

7. Emails from us

  • Service emails: booking confirmations, reminders, your Opportunity Brief and a follow-up about next steps. These are part of the service you requested.
  • Marketing emails: occasional insights, resources and offers. We send these only if you opted in. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe by writing to hello@testerpartner.com.

8. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses only the cookies needed to work and stay secure, plus analytics cookies if you allow them.

  • Strictly necessary: set by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) for security and performance. They do not require consent.
  • Analytics (optional): Google Analytics 4 cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*), which can last up to 2 years. We set them only after you accept analytics cookies where consent is required. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie settings on our website.

We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies, and we do not send personal information such as your name or email to Google Analytics.

10. International transfers

TesterPartner is operated from Argentina, and our service providers process information mainly in the United States and in other countries where they operate. These countries may have data protection laws different from those of your country.

When you submit your brief, you agree that your information may be transferred and processed in those countries. For information about people in the European Union, the United Kingdom or other regions with transfer rules, we rely on the safeguards offered by our providers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or certification under recognized data privacy frameworks.

12. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including access limited to the people who need it, two-factor authentication on our accounts and providers with established security programs. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete your information
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information
  • Receive your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (for recordings, marketing or analytics), without affecting what we did before
  • File a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@testerpartner.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request. We respond within the time limits set by applicable law (for example, within 10 calendar days for access requests under Argentine law). We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

You can contact the authority in your country: in Argentina, the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP); in the European Union or the United Kingdom, your local data protection authority; in the United States, the privacy regulator or Attorney General of your state.

Notice required under Argentine law

El titular de los datos personales tiene la facultad de ejercer el derecho de acceso a los mismos en forma gratuita a intervalos no inferiores a seis meses, salvo que se acredite un interés legítimo al efecto conforme lo establecido en el artículo 14, inciso 3 de la Ley Nº 25.326. La AGENCIA DE ACCESO A LA INFORMACIÓN PÚBLICA, en su carácter de Órgano de Control de la Ley Nº 25.326, tiene la atribución de atender las denuncias y reclamos que interpongan quienes resulten afectados en sus derechos por incumplimiento de las normas vigentes en materia de protección de datos personales.

In English: you may access your personal data free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless you show a legitimate interest in accessing it sooner (Article 14(3), Law 25,326). The AAIP, as the supervisory authority under Law 25,326, handles complaints from anyone whose data protection rights have been affected.
