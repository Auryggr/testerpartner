Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Publication checklist: Before publishing, confirm the legal identity and jurisdiction of the service operator, the actual vendors and retention periods, and the recording and analytics consent implementation. This draft must reflect the live system.

1. Who operates this service

TesterPartner provides preliminary research and mapping sessions for ecommerce growth decisions. For privacy questions, contact hello@testerpartner.com. The legal name and registered address of the responsible operator must be added before this policy is finalized.

2. Information we collect

When you submit a brief or book a session, we may collect your name, email, business or client website, your answers about a growth challenge, scheduling information, and related correspondence. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential customer lists, or sensitive personal information.

3. Why we use it

We use submitted information to respond to your request, schedule and administer a session, prepare relevant preliminary research, communicate with you, maintain service reliability, and document agreed next steps. We do not promise that public research will produce a conclusive answer.

4. Research and third-party services

Preliminary research may analyze publicly available customer commentary and market signals. Our operations may involve Cloudflare for website hosting, Google Calendar and related Google services for scheduling, Airtable for records, and Laxis for meeting transcription when used. Research processing may use GitHub-hosted workflows. Data shared with a provider should be limited to what is necessary for its role. Confirm the actual provider configuration and geographic processing before publishing.

5. Meetings, recording and transcripts

Where a meeting is recorded or transcribed, participants should receive clear notice and any legally required choice or consent before recording begins. Recording and transcription are not necessary to read this website. We will not publish identifiable meeting content as marketing material without separate authorization.

6. Analytics and cookies

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand website visits, referral sources and interactions. Analytics settings, consent requirements, and cookie controls must be configured appropriately before activation. Do not include personal information in analytics events or URLs. If analytics cookies require prior consent in a visitor's jurisdiction, they should not be set before that consent is obtained.

7. Sharing and security

We do not sell submitted personal information. Service providers may process information to help us operate the website, schedule sessions, maintain records or prepare research. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and your choices

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the stated purposes, applicable obligations and legitimate recordkeeping. A specific retention schedule must be confirmed before publication. To request access, correction or deletion, email hello@testerpartner.com. Requests are handled subject to applicable law.

9. International processing and changes

Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to applicable safeguards. We may update this policy when our services or processing practices change, and will revise the date above.
